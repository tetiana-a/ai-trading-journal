01

Curriculum

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Market Foundations

Build the chart-reading base before adding indicators or complex setups.

  • Uptrend, downtrend and range
  • Support, resistance, breakout and retest
  • Chart structures and candle patterns
  • Higher-timeframe context
02 / TECHNICAL

Technical Analysis

Use indicators as confirmation, not as a substitute for price structure.

  • SMA and directional context
  • RSI and overbought / oversold
  • MACD and momentum
  • Divergence and two-scenario planning
03 / RISK

Risk Management

Protect capital first. Every trade needs invalidation, size and planned risk.

  • Predefined risk per trade
  • Stop loss and position sizing
  • Risk / reward planning
  • Emotional and revenge-trading controls
04 / FUNDAMENTALS

Fundamental Analysis

Connect chart setups with macro, project news and market-wide risk context.

  • Economic calendar
  • Crypto market headlines
  • Token / project fundamentals
  • News-driven volatility and liquidations
05 / SMC

Smart Money Concepts

Study liquidity and structure as a framework for understanding where reactions may occur.

  • POI and order blocks
  • Liquidity pools and sweeps
  • Imbalance, BOS and CHOCH
  • Supply / demand, Fibonacci and Wyckoff
06 / PROCESS

Trading Workflow

Turn analysis into a repeatable decision process that can be measured and improved.

  • Macro → asset → chart context
  • Bullish and bearish scenarios
  • Entry, stop, target and sizing
  • Journal and weekly review
07 / READING

Reading Notes

Personal summaries from trading literature and coursework, organized around execution and discipline.

  • Trend and levels
  • Trading systems
  • Stop loss / take profit
  • Discipline, review and risk
PRACTICE

Chart Drills

Learning becomes useful only when concepts are annotated and reviewed on real charts.

  • Mark POIs and order blocks
  • Find liquidity sweeps
  • Identify BOS / CHOCH
  • Review A+ and failed setups
02

Trading Workflow

01 · ContextMacro events, market sentiment, project-specific news.
02 · StructureTrend, levels, liquidity, supply / demand, structure.
03 · PlanEntry, stop, target, position size and invalidation.
04 · ExecuteWait for confirmation. Avoid forcing trades.
05 · ReviewJournal the trade, screenshot it, review process quality.
Core principle: good process ≠ guaranteed profit. The journal exists to separate decision quality from short-term outcome and to make recurring mistakes visible.
03

Use it with the Journal

Before the trade

Write the thesis, define the invalidation point, calculate risk and save the setup screenshot.

After the trade

Record the result, emotion and lesson. Then use AI analysis as a second opinion on process quality — not as a signal service.
