# 04 — Fundamental Analysis

Technical analysis should not be separated completely from market context.

## What to check

Before a trade, review:

- major macroeconomic events,
- high-impact US economic data,
- central-bank decisions,
- market-wide crypto news,
- project-specific news,
- unusual risk-off / risk-on moves,
- token-specific fundamentals where relevant.

## Why news matters

A possible chain during a high-volatility event:

1. Weak or surprising macro data appears.
2. Risk assets react.
3. Large participants reduce exposure.
4. Key technical support breaks.
5. Stops and liquidations accelerate the move.
6. Liquidity is taken.
7. Price may later stabilize or reverse.

This is why trading directly into major news is risky.

## Crypto project checklist

For a token or protocol, research:

- what problem the project solves,
- token utility,
- supply and unlock schedule,
- development activity,
- ecosystem adoption,
- major partnerships,
- regulatory risks,
- liquidity,
- exchange availability,
- competitor landscape.

## Practical routine

Before opening a position:

- check the economic calendar,
- check broad crypto headlines,
- check the project's latest news,
- then return to the chart.

Fundamentals provide context; the chart provides execution structure.
