# 01 — Market Foundations

## Market direction

A simple starting point is to classify price action into:

- **Uptrend** — higher highs and higher lows.
- **Downtrend** — lower highs and lower lows.
- **Range / flat** — price rotates between support and resistance.
- **Long-term trend** — broader directional context.
- **Short-term trend** — local move inside the larger structure.

A trade idea should make sense in both the local and higher-timeframe context.

## Key levels

Important areas to mark before considering an entry:

- **Support**
- **Resistance**
- **Psychological levels**
- **Breakout levels**
- **Retest levels**

A level is not an automatic trade signal. It is an area where price behavior becomes more important.

## Basic chart structures

Common chart patterns used for practice:

- Head and Shoulders
- Double Top
- Double Bottom
- Ascending Triangle
- Flag

Patterns should be treated as context plus confirmation, not as standalone guarantees.

## Candlestick patterns

Examples:

- Hammer
- Hanging Man
- Engulfing
- Doji
- Morning Star

The useful question is not only *which candle appeared*, but **where it appeared and what the surrounding structure says**.

## Study rule

> Structure first, signal second.

The same candle or indicator can mean very different things depending on trend, level, volatility, and market context.
