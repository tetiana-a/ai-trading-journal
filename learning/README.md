# Trading Learning Hub

This folder contains structured study notes that support the **AI Trading Journal** project.

The goal is practical: turn theory into a repeatable process for market analysis, risk control, trade planning, journaling, and post-trade review.

## How this connects to the app

The journal is designed to reinforce this process:

**Context → Setup → Risk → Execution → Review**

For every trade, the useful questions are:

- What is the higher-timeframe market context?
- What level or setup justified the entry?
- What invalidates the idea?
- What is the planned risk/reward?
- Was the trade executed according to plan?
- What can be improved next time?

## Important

The repository intentionally contains **original summaries and study notes**, not redistributed copies of third-party books or paid course files.
