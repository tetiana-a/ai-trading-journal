# 06 — Trading Workflow

This is the practical workflow that connects the study material to the journal.

## Step 1 — Macro context

Check:

- economic calendar,
- high-impact US events,
- broad market sentiment,
- major crypto news.

Avoid entering just before major volatility events unless that is explicitly part of the strategy.

## Step 2 — Project / asset context

Review:

- project news,
- token-specific developments,
- unusual volume or volatility,
- major support/resistance from higher timeframes.

## Step 3 — Chart analysis

Mark:

- trend,
- key levels,
- market structure,
- liquidity,
- supply/demand,
- order blocks / POIs,
- relevant indicators.

## Step 4 — Build two scenarios

### Scenario A
What confirms the bullish case?

### Scenario B
What confirms the bearish case?

Set alerts instead of staring at the chart.

## Step 5 — Model the trade

Before entry:

- entry,
- stop,
- target,
- position size,
- R:R,
- reason,
- invalidation.

If the trade does not meet the minimum quality threshold, skip it.

## Step 6 — Execute on demo first

A useful learning sequence:

1. Create a demo account.
2. Take a small sample of planned trades.
3. Record every trade.
4. Review process quality, not only PnL.
5. Adjust one variable at a time.

## Step 7 — Journal immediately

For every trade, capture:

- market,
- direction,
- timeframe,
- setup,
- entry,
- exit,
- stop,
- target,
- screenshot,
- emotion,
- thesis,
- result,
- lesson.

## Step 8 — Post-trade review

Classify the trade:

- good process / good outcome,
- good process / bad outcome,
- bad process / good outcome,
- bad process / bad outcome.

Only the process can be improved consistently.

## Weekly review

At the end of the week, check:

- win rate,
- average R,
- profit factor,
- most common mistake,
- best-performing setup,
- emotional mistakes,
- rule violations,
- screenshots of A+ setups.

The AI Trading Journal is intended to make this review faster and more objective.
