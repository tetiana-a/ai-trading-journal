## Alexander Gerchik — *Course of the Active Trader*

Personal study topics taken from the book include:

- trend identification,
- support and resistance,
- trading patterns,
- candlestick analysis,
- stop loss and take profit,
- liquidity,
- long and short mechanics,
- technical versus fundamental analysis,
- futures basics,
- risk management,
- trader discipline.

The key lesson is that a trading system is not only an entry pattern. It is a complete decision framework:

**setup + risk + execution + discipline + review**

This repository does not redistribute the book itself. These are personal study notes and takeaways.

## Recommended use

Do not try to learn every concept at once.

A better progression:

1. Market structure
2. Levels
3. Risk
4. One setup
5. Journal
6. Review 20–30 trades
7. Only then add more concepts

Complexity should be earned by evidence, not added because a chart looks interesting.
